# Weekly clipping rubric

## Importance

Importance is the value of reviewing an item for business decisions this week.

| Rating | Guideline |
| --- | --- |
| High | Verified major contract, customer adoption, product launch or pricing change, funding, acquisition, earnings event, or policy decision with material business impact |
| Medium | Specific partnership, customer example, feature expansion, market entry, or organizational change where scale or execution is still limited |
| Low | Routine promotion, event attendance, generic interview, or announcement with little new evidence or decision value |
| Pending | Source access or factual evidence is insufficient |

## Threat

Threat is the concrete competitive pressure that an event could create for the monitored organization.

| Rating | Guideline |
| --- | --- |
| High | Verified win in an overlapping customer or procurement opportunity, demonstrated substitutable product advantage, exclusive access, or repeated execution with clear impact |
| Medium | Adjacent-market entry or possible substitute capability without evidence of customer conversion or material impact |
| Low | Limited overlap or an exploratory signal with little near-term effect |
| Pending | The evidence does not establish the monitored organization's capability or the competitor's execution |

## Evidence fields

- **Tone:** Favorable, neutral, or critical, with a short source-based reason. A republished press release is not independent praise.
- **Key message:** Separate verified developments from the source's own claims.
- **Differentiation:** Compare capabilities only where public evidence supports the comparison.
- **Policy or trend:** Include only a documented connection to an industry or policy change.
- **Threat signal:** Note observed customer overlap, contract, price, substitution, policy access, or execution evidence.
- **Next step:** Label a proposed action as a recommendation, not as a verified fact.

Importance and threat are separate judgments. A high importance rating does not imply high threat. State the evidence for each rating and use Pending when the source does not support a reliable conclusion.
