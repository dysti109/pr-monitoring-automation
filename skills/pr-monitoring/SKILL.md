# PR monitoring

Create an evidence-based monitoring brief for the organization and search targets
configured by the user. Treat configured entities as search targets, not as an
exclusive source list for finding comparable businesses.

## Collect and verify

Use configured search terms and feeds to find candidates. Google News RSS,
Naver News when accessible, and general web search are discovery tools; they do
not prove that a candidate is relevant or within the requested period.

For every retained item, verify the publisher or channel, headline, original
publication time, and the connection to the monitored organization or target.
Prefer the original publisher page and primary public records when available.
If a page or search source is blocked, record the limitation and do not describe
the search as complete.

Use three distinct discovery passes when the workflow is configured for
precollection: organization and brand names; products, services, customers, and
business events; then aliases, spelling variants, and long-tail terms. Combine
the candidate sets, deduplicate the same URL, and check the full period for new
items during final verification.

## Include and exclude

- Group A covers the monitored organization and its brands. Include its
  official releases and third-party coverage. Online posts are included only
  when allowed by local configuration and when the connection is verified.
- Group B covers configured competitors. Include third-party reporting that
  directly covers the company's business, product, customer, contract,
  performance, or partnership. Exclude self-promotional company posts and
  passing mentions.
- Group C covers other organizations with a documented overlap in industry,
  service, customer group, or technology. Include third-party reporting and
  state the overlap rationale. The discovery list is not fixed.

Apply A > B > C when an item fits more than one group. Exclude advertising,
routine personnel notices and obituaries, unrelated namesakes, out-of-period
items, and articles that only list an organization without substantive coverage.
A company press release republished by a media outlet may be included when it
meets the criteria; label the source relationship.

Keep distinct articles from different outlets. Group coverage of the same event
into one issue with a representative article and links to additional coverage.
Count issues separately from article or post exposures.

## Assess

- Importance measures how much the item matters to the monitored
  organization's business decisions, based on relevance, evidence, and impact.
- Threat measures the concrete adverse competitive pressure on the monitored
  organization. Assess it separately from importance and tone.
- Reputation tone and risk apply to content about the monitored organization.
  Distinguish verified facts from claims, personal opinions, and inference.

Use the original publication time to enforce the configured KST window. Do not
infer contract awards, customer adoption, revenue, or product availability from
an ambiguous announcement. When evidence is insufficient, use a pending or
uncertain label and explain what remains unverified.

## Write the brief

Write concise Korean summaries with source links and publication times. Include
the collection group, source type, importance and threat with short reasons,
and reputation signals where applicable. State the actual search limitations,
such as blocked sources or unverifiable publication times. Never infer a
reporter's identity or contact details.
